In newer and upcoming mobile MMORPG releases, developers are heavily leaning into highly interactive couple and marriage systems that feature beautiful physical interaction emotes like the bridal carry (often referred to in anime/gaming communities as the "princess carry").
The top free-to-play mobile MMORPGs featuring this couple interaction include:

Featured Games with Bridal Carry Systems

Where Winds Meet Mobile (https://www.google.com/search?kgmid=FAILED_OR_SKIPPED&q=Where+Winds+Meet+Mobile)
An open-world Eastern martial arts (Wuxia) MMORPG that has taken the mobile and PC space by storm. It features incredibly deep social systems, including a formal Partnership system.
• How to get the pose: You must travel to the Crimson Cliff area with your partner, establish an official partnership, and earn points to unlock the iconic Bridal Carry emote from the Partnership Shop.
• Status: Free-to-play, cross-platform mobile/PC.

Mabinogi Mobile (https://www.google.com/search?kgmid=%2Fg%2F11g0wgsnw3&q=Mabinogi+Mobile)
Android
Nexon’s cozy anime open-world MMORPG that focuses just as much on lifestyle, dressing up, and relationships as it does on combat. It has gained massive popularity among couples for its highly detailed physical interactions.
• How to get the pose: The game features a beautifully smooth, dedicated bridal carry interaction that allows players to lift their friends or partners horizontally and run around together. It also features synchronized partner dancing and couple home decoration.
• Status: Launched globally, Free-to-play.

Sky: Children of the Light (https://www.google.com/search?kgmid=%2Fg%2F11gbk0z94r&q=Sky%3A+Children+of+the+Light)
2019 iOS +5
While structured more as a cozy, social adventure MMORPG rather than a traditional stats-and-gear grinder, it is widely regarded as one of the best and most romantic games for couples on mobile.
• How to get the pose: The Princess Carry / Bridal Carry is an unlockable tier-2 interactive friendship emote originally introduced via the Season of the Nine-Colored Deer. When activated, your partner sweeps you up into their arms and can carry you across the map. While carried, you can hold up a candle to give your partner infinite flying energy.
• Status: Free-to-play on iOS, Android, and consoles.
