Ghost of Tsushima (Sucker Punch Productions — Bellevue, Washington)

• Jin Sakai — 境井 仁. Created by the American studio Sucker Punch, Jin's surname combines 境 ("border") and 井 ("well"), while his given name 仁 means "benevolence" or "humanity"—one of the core Confucian virtues of Bushido.
• Yuna — ゆな. Jin's thief-turned-ally uses hiragana (ゆな) in the official Japanese script rather than kanji, reflecting her humble commoner origins outside the samurai class.
• Lord Shimura — 志村. Jin's uncle and strict samurai mentor carries the traditional surname 志村 ("aspiration village").

Overwatch (Blizzard Entertainment — Irvine, California)

• Hanzo Shimada — 島田 半蔵. While Blizzard's Japanese localization prints his name in katakana (ハンゾー) for readability, in-game Hanamura map graffiti confirms the kanji 半蔵—a direct nod to the legendary historical samurai and ninja Hattori Hanzō.
• Genji Shimada — 島田 源氏. Genji's name uses the kanji 源氏, referencing the famous Minamoto clan and the classic literary work The Tale of Genji (Genji Monogatari).
• Kiriko Kamori — 家守 霧子. In the Japanese release of Overwatch 2, Kiriko's name is officially written as 家守 霧子, combining 霧 ("mist/fog") and 子 ("child") with a surname meaning "house protector."

Mortal Kombat (Midway / NetherRealm Studios — Chicago, Illinois)

• Hanzo Hasashi (Scorpion) — 波佐志 半蔵. Co-created by Ed Boon and John Tobias in Chicago, Scorpion's real name uses the surname 波佐志 and given name 半蔵 (also inspired by Hattori Hanzō).
• Kenshi Takahashi — 高橋 健志. The blind telekinetic swordsman created for Mortal Kombat: Deadly Alliance bears the common Japanese surname 高橋 ("high bridge") and given name 健志 ("healthy/strong will").
