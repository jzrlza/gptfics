Notable Seasons & Moments

Fresh Pretty Cure! (https://www.google.com/search?kgmid=%2Fm%2F05f3h1j&q=Fresh+Pretty+Cure%21)
2009·1 season 8.3/10 · Hulu +3
Known by fans as a standout season for this trope, frequently featuring Cure Berry (https://www.google.com/search?kgmid=%2Fm%2F0ngb__v&q=pretty+cure+all+moments+the+cure+girls+carrying+boys+cure+berry) and Cure Peach (https://www.google.com/search?kgmid=%2Fm%2F010bx7mq&q=pretty+cure+all+moments+the+cure+girls+carrying+boys+cure+peach) bridal-carrying male characters like Daisuke and Soular (https://www.google.com/search?kgmid=%2Fm%2F010by5b_&q=pretty+cure+all+moments+the+cure+girls+carrying+boys+soular) out of danger.
Futari wa Pretty Cure (https://www.google.com/search?kgmid=%2Fm%2F03vcn5&q=Futari+wa+Pretty+Cure)
2004·1 season 6.9/10 · Hulu +5
Cure Black (https://www.google.com/search?kgmid=%2Fm%2F0njys50&q=pretty+cure+all+moments+the+cure+girls+carrying+boys+cure+black) famously rescues and carries her crush, Shougo Fujimura (https://www.google.com/search?kgmid=%2Fm%2F0p41llj&q=pretty+cure+all+moments+the+cure+girls+carrying+boys+shougo+fujimura), showcasing the franchise's early emphasis on physical strength.
KiraKira☆Pretty Cure A La Mode (https://www.google.com/search?kgmid=%2Fg%2F11c5s5cf0s&q=KiraKira%E2%98%86Pretty+Cure+A+La+Mode)
2017·1 season 6.8/10 · Hulu +2
Cure Whip carries Pikario (Julio) in a memorable rescue scene during his redemption arc.
Go! Princess Pretty Cure (https://www.google.com/search?kgmid=%2Fm%2F012c5y3k&q=Go%21+Princess+Pretty+Cure)
Cure Flora (https://www.google.com/search?kgmid=%2Fg%2F11fk09scj6&q=pretty+cure+all+moments+the+cure+girls+carrying+boys+cure+flora) and Cure Mermaid (https://www.google.com/search?kgmid=%2Fg%2F11h5q7d896&q=pretty+cure+all+moments+the+cure+girls+carrying+boys+cure+mermaid) both have heroic moments rescuing and carrying male allies, including Prince Kanata (https://www.google.com/search?kgmid=%2Fg%2F11h5rq3c2x&q=pretty+cure+all+moments+the+cure+girls+carrying+boys+prince+kanata) and Kuroro (https://www.google.com/search?kgmid=%2Fg%2F11fk1cjc4t&q=pretty+cure+all+moments+the+cure+girls+carrying+boys+kuroro).
Suite PreCure♪ (https://www.google.com/search?kgmid=%2Fm%2F0dsb65g&q=Suite+PreCure%E2%99%AA)
2011·1 season 7.7/10 · Hulu +3
Cure Melody (https://www.google.com/search?kgmid=%2Fm%2F0_z81kp&q=pretty+cure+all+moments+the+cure+girls+carrying+boys+cure+melody) and Cure Rhythm (https://www.google.com/search?kgmid=%2Fm%2F0_z81m7&q=pretty+cure+all+moments+the+cure+girls+carrying+boys+cure+rhythm) repeatedly perform dramatic rescues carrying male supporting characters away from battle.
Hirogaru Sky! Pretty Cure (https://www.google.com/search?kgmid=%2Fg%2F11scjsfvyf&q=Hirogaru+Sky%21+Pretty+Cure)
2023·1 season 6.9/10 · Hulu +3
While Cure Wing (https://www.google.com/search?kgmid=%2Fg%2F11kb1crt1f&q=pretty+cure+all+moments+the+cure+girls+carrying+boys+cure+wing) (the franchise's first main-team male Cure) is nicknamed "the princess carrier" for carrying the girls, Cure Sky (https://www.google.com/search?kgmid=%2Fg%2F11tsdrmdkh&q=pretty+cure+all+moments+the+cure+girls+carrying+boys+cure+sky) and Cure Butterfly (https://www.google.com/search?kgmid=%2Fg%2F11vkgc61rj&q=pretty+cure+all+moments+the+cure+girls+carrying+boys+cure+butterfly) also lift and carry Tsubasa during high-flying rescues.
Wonderful PreCure! (https://www.google.com/search?kgmid=%2Fg%2F11vlxjw0vt&q=Wonderful+PreCure%21)
2024·1 season 6.7/10 · ABEMA +5
Cure Wonderful (https://www.google.com/search?kgmid=%2Fg%2F11wx80c3rs&q=pretty+cure+all+moments+the+cure+girls+carrying+boys+cure+wonderful) and Cure Friendy (https://www.google.com/search?kgmid=%2Fg%2F11vq2zxh5x&q=pretty+cure+all+moments+the+cure+girls+carrying+boys+cure+friendy) regularly scoop up Satoru to keep him safe during monster encounters.
