Notable Seasons & Moments

• Fresh Pretty Cure!: Known by fans as a standout season for this trope, frequently featuring Cure Berry and Cure Peach bridal-carrying male characters like Daisuke and Soular out of danger.
• Futari wa Pretty Cure: Cure Black famously rescues and carries her crush, Shougo Fujimura, showcasing the franchise's early emphasis on physical strength.
• KiraKira☆Pretty Cure A La Mode: Cure Whip carries Pikario (Julio) in a memorable rescue scene during his redemption arc.
• Go! Princess Pretty Cure: Cure Flora and Cure Mermaid both have heroic moments rescuing and carrying male allies, including Prince Kanata and Kuroro.
• Suite PreCure♪: Cure Melody and Cure Rhythm repeatedly perform dramatic rescues carrying male supporting characters away from battle.
• Hirogaru Sky! Pretty Cure: While Cure Wing (the franchise's first main-team male Cure) is nicknamed "the princess carrier" for carrying the girls, Cure Sky and Cure Butterfly also lift and carry Tsubasa during high-flying rescues.
• Wonderful PreCure!: Cure Wonderful and Cure Friendy regularly scoop up Satoru to keep him safe during monster encounters.
